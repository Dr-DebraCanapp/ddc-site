§ Verification

Verify a certificate.

Every certificate of continuing education issued for the Canine Diagnostic Musculoskeletal Ultrasound course carries a number in the form DDC-2026-A1B2C3. Enter it below to confirm who it was issued to, for what, and when.

Certificates are issued on completion of a module examination, and on completion of the full curriculum. A certificate number confirms only the details printed on that certificate. For anything further — scope of training, or confirmation of the remote read qualification — write to info@drdebracanapp.com.
