Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the practice collects, why, and what happens to it. It covers this website, the referral portal, the course, and the text and email alerts the practice sends.

Last updated 10 August 2026

Who this covers

This policy applies to the veterinary practice of Dr. Debra A. Canapp, DVM, DACVSMR (“the practice”, “we”), operating at drdebracanapp.com and portal.drdebracanapp.com.

Questions about anything here go to info@drdebracanapp.com.

What we collect

Website visitors

If you send us a message through a contact link, we receive whatever you write and the email address you send it from. Forms on the site are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a submission comes from a person rather than an automated script.

Referring veterinary practices and their veterinarians

When a practice applies for portal access we collect the practice name and address, the veterinarian's name, professional credentials, email address and telephone number. Once an account exists we hold the case material submitted through it: patient records, diagnostic images, clinical histories, the questions asked and the reports issued in reply, together with invoices, statements and payment records.

Course participants

Name, email address, professional details, homework submissions and the images accompanying them, and a record of enrollment, access period and any extensions.

Text and email alert recipients

Name, mobile number and email address, and a log of which alerts were sent and whether they were delivered.

Patient records and clinical images

Diagnostic images and patient records submitted for remote interpretation are clinical records. They are retained as part of the case file, associated with the submitting practice, and used to produce the report, to answer follow-up questions, and to support the continuity of care of that animal.

They are not sold, shared for marketing, or made available to other practices. Case material may be used in teaching or publication only in de-identified form and only where the submitting practice has agreed.

Text messages

We do not share mobile numbers with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers collected for alerts are used only to send those alerts, and are passed only to the messaging provider that delivers them.

Message frequency varies and depends on activity. Alerts are event-driven rather than scheduled, and are typically fewer than twenty messages a month.

Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them, or write to info@drdebracanapp.com and we will remove the number. Reply HELP for assistance.

Alerts are sent to the practice's own staff about operational events, such as a case arriving or a referring veterinarian asking a question. Consent is given directly by each recipient when they provide their number.

How we use information

  • To provide the service: interpreting studies, issuing reports, scheduling clinic days, answering questions.
  • To operate accounts, including sign-in, invitations and password resets.
  • To bill for work and record what has been paid.
  • To send operational notifications about the progress of a case.
  • To deliver and administer the course.
  • To keep the records a veterinary practice is expected to keep.

We do not sell personal information. We do not send marketing email to referring veterinarians; the emails they receive concern their own cases and accounts.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the service. Each receives only what it needs to do its part:

  • Supabase — hosts the database, accounts and stored files behind the portal.
  • GitHub Pages — serves this website and the portal.
  • Cloudflare — the Turnstile check on public forms.
  • Twilio — delivers text alerts.
  • Resend — delivers email notifications.
  • Stripe — processes card payments, when card payment is offered. Card details are entered with Stripe and are never held by the practice.
  • Anthropic — where an assisted reading of submitted intake text is used, that text is processed by Anthropic's API. It is not used to train models.

Cookies

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The portal stores a sign-in session in your browser so that you stay signed in, and remembers small display preferences such as your language choice. Clearing your browser storage removes them and signs you out.

How long we keep things

Clinical records, reports, invoices and statements are retained as business and clinical records. Accounts that are no longer needed are deactivated rather than deleted, so that the case history and billing record remain intact and attributable. Alert recipients can be removed at any time, which stops future messages.

Your choices

  • Progress emails — referring veterinarians can switch off the intermediate case-progress emails. The finished report is always sent.
  • Text alerts — reply STOP, or ask us to remove the number.
  • Access and correction — write to us and we will tell you what we hold about you and correct anything that is wrong.
  • Deletion — we will delete what we are not required to retain. Clinical and financial records generally must be kept.

Security

Access to the portal requires an account issued by the practice. Staff accounts use two-factor authentication. Data is held by the providers listed above under their own security controls, and access within the practice is limited to the people who need it.

Children

The service is intended for veterinary professionals and adult course participants. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes affecting portal users will be notified by email.
