§ 05.1 How it works

Three steps, no interruption to your access.

Extensions are handled directly rather than through an automated checkout, so an extension can be applied even after an access window has closed.

Step 01

Tell us which one

Use the button above, or write to info@drdebracanapp.com with the name and email address you enrolled under.

Step 02

Access is extended

Your access window moves out by 3 or 6 months from its current expiry — not from the date you ask — so nothing is lost by requesting it early.

Step 03

An invoice follows

Payable by check or bank transfer under the terms on your original enrollment.

Card payment online isn't available yet. Extensions are arranged by email and invoiced, as above. Card payment is being added — if you would prefer to pay by card, say so when you write and we will send a link once it is live.

Extensions are stackable and can be added at any point during your access window, including immediately before expiration. Homework grading and remote-read submissions during the extended period are billed per the original enrollment terms. All pricing in USD.
