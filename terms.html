Remote imaging interpretation

A report is a consultative professional opinion based on the images and history supplied. It is not a diagnosis of the animal, and it does not create a veterinarian–patient–client relationship between the practice and the animal or its owner. That relationship remains with the attending veterinarian, who is responsible for examining the patient, for clinical decisions, and for treatment.

The quality of a report depends on the quality of what is submitted. Where a study cannot be read, we notify the submitting practice and a return fee applies in place of the read fee.

Standard turnaround is as published in the fee schedule. A STAT read carries a written guarantee of a report within 24 hours of our receiving a readable study — but the requesting practice must contact us directly so that we can acknowledge the STAT request. Submitting a case marked STAT does not on its own start the 24-hour period. Where a study cannot be read, the STAT fee is not charged.

In-person clinic days

Clinic days are scheduled with the host hospital in advance. The host hospital is responsible for the facility, for restraint and handling, for any sedation, and for owner consent. Cancellations, no-shows and out-of-region travel may carry the charges set out in the fee schedule.

Fees and payment

Fees are those in force on the date the work is performed, per the published rate card and any pricing agreed with an individual hospital. Historical work is priced at the rates in force on its own date.

Invoices for in-person clinic days are issued per day. Remote reads are billed on a monthly statement per practice. Default terms are net 15 days unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Where card payment is offered, the amount payable by card may differ from the amount payable by check or bank transfer. Both figures are shown on the invoice. Accounts in arrears may have portal access or further case acceptance suspended until the balance is cleared.

The course

Enrollment includes 12 months of access from the date of enrollment, covering lectures, video labs, anatomy reference material and homework submission. Access may be extended in 3- or 6-month intervals for the published fee; extensions are stackable and may be added at any point, including after an access window has closed.

Homework grading and remote-read submissions during an extended period are billed on the original enrollment terms. Course fees are for access and instruction; they are not contingent on any particular examination or qualification outcome.

The remote-read qualification pathway recognises competence in submitting studies for interpretation. It is not a specialty credential, a licence, or a substitute for board certification.
