§ 05.1 How it works

Three steps, no interruption to your access.

An extension can be added at any point, including after an access window has closed. Nothing is lost by arranging it early.

Step 01

Choose your extension

Pick 3 or 6 months above and pay by bank transfer or card. Use the same email address you enrolled under.

Step 02

Access is extended

Your access window moves out by 3 or 6 months from its current expiry — not from the date you ask — so nothing is lost by requesting it early.

Step 03

Pay and you're done

Bank transfer, card, or check if you prefer. Bank transfer takes a few days to clear; card is immediate.

Prefer to pay by check, or would rather arrange it by email? Write to info@drdebracanapp.com with the name and email address you enrolled under, and we will invoice you instead.
